Privacy Policy

Tumbletub · last updated November 10, 2026

Tumbletub is a puzzle game. It does not ask who you are, and it does not sell any data about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: stars per level, the Rewind and Extra Fizz helpers in stock, and which store purchases were already delivered.
  • Your settings: the Sound switch.
  • Whether you have bought the Laundry Room, Kitchen Sink, Garden Pool or Whole House rooms.

This is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Purchases

The Bathtub and its 24 levels are free. Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
