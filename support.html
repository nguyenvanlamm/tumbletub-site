How to play

  1. 1
    Place.

    Tap a glowing ring on a bath toy to place a numbered fizz ball. Place one, or several.

  2. 2
    Fizz!

    Press Fizz! The balls pop in number order, and the foam pushes everything nearby away from each ball.

  3. 3
    Plop.

    Get every toy into the water. Anything that lands on the floor loses the level, and a piece marked with a crossed-out drop must stay dry.

Helpers:

  • Start over is always free.
  • Rewind undoes the last Fizz!
  • Extra Fizz gives you one more ball.
  • Rewind and Extra Fizz are earned by clearing levels 6, 12, 18 and 24 of a room for the first time. They are never sold.

Purchases

The Bathtub and its 24 levels are free. The Laundry Room, Kitchen Sink and Garden Pool rooms (24 levels each) are one-time purchases (non-consumable): $1.99 each. The Whole House gets all 3 paid rooms for $3.99, saving $1.98, and is offered only before you buy any room. The App Store shows prices in your currency. Bought something before, or on another device with the same store account? Open Settings → Restore purchases or Shop → Restore purchases.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
