How to play

Tap a glowing ring on a bath toy to place a numbered fizz ball. Place one or more, then press Fizz! — the balls pop in number order, and the foam pushes everything nearby away from each ball. Get every toy into the water. Anything that lands on the floor loses the level, and a piece marked with a crossed-out drop must stay dry. Start over is always free. Rewind (undo the last Fizz!) and Extra Fizz (one more ball) are earned by clearing levels 6, 12, 18 and 24 of a room for the first time.

Purchases

The Bathtub and its 24 levels are free. The Laundry Room, Kitchen Sink and Garden Pool rooms (24 levels each) are one-time purchases (non-consumable): $1.99 each, or the Whole House for $3.99 while you own no room yet. The App Store shows prices in your currency. Bought something before, or on another device with the same store account? Open Settings → Restore purchases or Shop → Restore purchases.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
